Simple Scrambled Eggs

A quick breakfast ready in 5 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper

Instructions

  1. Crack eggs into a bowl and whisk until well combined.
  2. Melt butter in a non-stick pan over medium heat.
  3. Pour in eggs and stir gently until they start to set.
  4. Add salt and pepper to taste.

Tips

  • Don't overcook or the eggs will become tough.
  • Remove from heat while they're still slightly undercooked for the best texture.
